## What you can do

- Say that your project is based on, compatible with, or works with Nexus or one of its apps, as long as it is true.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to the official project, for example in an article or a review.
- Link to this repository and show its name when you describe where the code came from.
- Use the name when you contribute to this repository or discuss it.

## What you cannot do

- Use our name or logo in a way that suggests your project or product is official, endorsed by us, or made by us.
- Use our name or logo as the name of your product, company, domain or social account.
- Use a name or logo that is close enough to ours to confuse people.
- Change our logo, or combine it with another mark.

## Forks and redistributed builds

If you publish a fork or a modified build, you must rename it and remove our logos and other branding.
This includes the app or tool name, the icon, the window title, the installer name and its background image, the store
listing, and the update feed.
Keep the copyright and license notice, as the MIT license requires. Keep the NOTICE file too: the third-party code it lists needs it.
Say plainly that your version is unofficial and point to this repository as its source.

You may keep the names of the `nexus-*` crates in this repository only where code
references them. Do not use them as the names of products you ship.

Official release packages are signed by us. A build you make yourself, or a package someone else signs, is not an official build.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, ask before you ship. Write to <trademarks@nexus.inc>.
