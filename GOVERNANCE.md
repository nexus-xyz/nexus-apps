# Governance

Nexus Desktop is maintained by Nexus Laboratories, Inc. This page says how decisions get made.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, cut releases and enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Maintainers are Nexus engineers, and the team reviews every pull request.

## How decisions are made

- Most changes are decided in pull requests. One maintainer's approval and passing checks are enough to merge.
- Larger changes (new features, big refactors, anything that changes how the app behaves) start as an issue.
  Maintainers and contributors discuss it there. The maintainers decide when the discussion ends.
- We look for agreement. When there is none, the maintainers at Nexus make the call, and they explain it in the issue.
- Changes to the license, the name and logo, or the release process are decided by Nexus alone.
- We favor changes that keep the app fast, simple and consistent with the existing code.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who do good work over time can be invited to become maintainers.
We look for:

- A record of merged contributions of good quality, with tests.
- Helpful, kind reviews of other people's work.
- Good judgment about scope and design.
- Following the Code of Conduct.

An existing maintainer can nominate you, or you can ask. The maintainers decide together.
A maintainer who is inactive for six months, or who asks to step down, becomes an emeritus contributor.
